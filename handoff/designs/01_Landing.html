The problem

HS coaches run programs on
Sheets, Word docs, and group texts.

A HS baseball coach manages 60+ kids across Varsity, JV, and Freshman. Tryouts in Excel. Practice plans in Word. Lineups on paper. Depth chart in a notes app. Parent updates by group text. No connection between any of it — and no record of a kid's development when they graduate.

PRACTICE

Plan in the office. Run it on the field.

Drag drills onto a timeline, assign coaches, hit print — or open on your phone for a live, timer-driven field runner. Your drill library grows every week.

  • Post-game notes tie back to individual player profiles
  • Historical lineups you can recall and adjust for the next matchup
PLAYER PROFILE

The part the kids actually love.

Every player on every Rostr roster gets a profile — their career, their metrics, their highlights, shareable with a link. It's the social layer baseball has never had. Kids want to use this, and that's what makes coaches' lives easier too.

  • Every roster spot links to a real profile — no more guessing who #21 is
  • Metrics, stats, and highlight reels — all in one feed
  • Year-over-year development charts that coaches love
  • Player-controlled privacy; verified badge shows data was entered by real coaches
  • Shareable link for college coaches — no more PDF attachments and Google Drive folders
OPPONENT SCOUTING

The network is the data.

When two Rostr programs play each other, both coaches see the shared scouting report. No more building opposing rosters by hand in GameChanger.

  • Auto-populated opposing lineup from shared Rostr data
  • Spray charts, tendencies, and strengths from prior meetings
  • Conference-wide opponent coverage grows as adoption grows
  • Private scouting notes stay with your program
MESSAGING

Kill the group texts. Keep the context.

Team-wide, position-group, parent-only, or 1:1 threads — all tied to player records. "Was that message about Noah?" One tap, see his profile.

  • Read receipts for critical announcements (practice canceled, bus late)
  • Threaded discussion on lineup decisions, tryout rankings
